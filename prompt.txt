I own a YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels brand called **TheMemeCanvas**.

I already have **hundreds of videos stored inside a Google Drive folder**.

This folder acts as my Content Library / Queue.

I DO NOT upload a new video every day.

Instead, the application must automatically publish **ONE video every day at exactly 9:00 PM (configurable)** until all queued videos have been uploaded.

The workflow should be:

Download temporarily

↓

Generate metadata using AI

↓

↓

↓

↓

Upload thumbnail if available

↓

Move file to Uploaded folder

↓

Save upload history

↓

Delete temporary files

↓

Notify me

Repeat automatically every day.

When the Queue folder becomes empty, notify me immediately and continue checking periodically for newly added videos.

The application should require virtually zero manual work after setup.
